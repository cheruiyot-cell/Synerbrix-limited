Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you make an informed decision about your business technology.

How fast can you respond to an emergency IT outage?

For urgent operational breakdowns within Nairobi, our remote team responds within 15–30 minutes, and on-site dispatches arrive within 2 hours depending on your service level agreement (SLA).

How much does a website design or custom software cost?

Pricing is project-based depending on feature complexity, integrations (such as M-Pesa or custom databases), and total page count. We issue a transparent, fixed quote upfront so you face zero surprise billing.

Can I view my CCTV security cameras remotely on my smartphone?

Yes. All CCTV installations include secure remote access configuration on iOS and Android devices, allowing you to view live HD footage and receive motion alerts wherever you have internet.

Do you offer hardware warranties for CCTV and network equipment?

Yes. All hardware equipment supplied by Synerbrix carries original manufacturer warranties (typically 1 to 3 years) alongside our service guarantee on cabling and labor workmanship.

How do estate or commercial Wi-Fi hotspot billing systems work?

We deploy managed routers and access points integrated with automated M-Pesa payment gateways. Users select time/data packages, pay via M-Pesa, and automatically receive instant internet access credentials.

Can we retain Synerbrix on a monthly managed IT retainer?

Yes. We offer customized monthly IT retainer packages for ongoing office support, network management, server backups, and website maintenance at a predictable monthly fee.
