Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers to help you make an informed decision about your business technology.

How fast can you respond to emergency IT outages or network failures?
For urgent operational breakdowns within Nairobi and surrounding regions, our remote helpdesk initiates troubleshooting within 15–30 minutes. If on-site technician dispatch is required, our emergency response team arrives within 2 hours under our standard Service Level Agreement (SLA).
How much does a website design or custom web software cost?
Pricing is transparent and project-based depending on your scope, page count, and functional requirements (such as M-Pesa payment gateways, customer portals, or database management). Following a free technical consultation, we deliver a fixed itemized quote upfront with zero hidden fees.
Can I view my CCTV security cameras remotely on my mobile phone?
Yes, absolutely. All 4K and HD CCTV installations by Synerbrix include secure remote access configuration on iOS and Android smartphones, tablets, and computers. You can stream live footage, review recorded history, and receive motion detection push alerts anytime, anywhere.
How does commercial Wi-Fi hotspot M-Pesa billing work?
We deploy enterprise-grade access points integrated with automated M-Pesa PayBill software (PayBill 400200). Users who connect to your Wi-Fi network are greeted with a branded captive portal where they select a time or data bundle, pay directly via M-Pesa, and receive instant internet access without manual intervention.
Do your hardware components and installations come with warranties?
Yes. All equipment supplied by Synerbrix (including CCTV cameras, network switches, routers, and computer parts) comes with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 3 years. Additionally, we provide a full workmanship guarantee on structured cabling, installation, and labor.
Can we retain Synerbrix for ongoing monthly IT maintenance?
Yes. We offer flexible Managed IT Retainer plans tailored to small and medium enterprises. This includes ongoing helpdesk support, proactive virus/security monitoring, automated cloud backups, network optimization, and regular hardware health checks for a predictable monthly fee.
What payment options do you accept for invoices?
We accept corporate M-Pesa PayBill payments using Business PayBill 400200 (Account Number: 0714202996) as well as direct Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) / Bank Transfer. All payments are officially receipted and accompanied by tax-compliant invoices.
